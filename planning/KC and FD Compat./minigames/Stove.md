# Stove

## Your design
No real minigame. On its own the stove isn't very useful, so keep it simple but make it more useful: use it for **long cooking** and **marinating** (if marinating gets added) - a plain UI where you put stuff in.

## Notes
- Both mods have stoves (`StoveBlock`, FD also `StoveBlockEntity`). Cook blocks like the pot sit on top of them.
- Probably also the natural place to require "is it actually lit" for the other minigames - currently they don't check heat at all.
- Low priority compared to [[Wok]] and the boards.
